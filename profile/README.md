# Liumy

**Liumy** is a creative software studio focused on crafting fast, reliable, and beautiful digital products.  
We build **websites, applications, plugins, and automation systems** that help businesses grow and perform better.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

Liumy provides end-to-end digital services and custom development, including:

- 🌐 **Web Development** – modern websites, landing pages, and CMS solutions  
- ⚙️ **App Development** – scalable, high-performance applications  
- 💡 **Automation & Integrations** – process optimization and custom APIs  
- 🎨 **Design & Branding** – UI/UX, identity systems, and visuals  
- 🔐 **Security & Performance** – optimized, maintainable, and protected systems  

Our focus is on **clean architecture, optimized performance, and long-term maintainability**.

---

## 🧠 Philosophy

We believe great software is:
- **Simple** – understandable and elegant  
- **Fast** – optimized for real-world performance  
- **Reliable** – built with attention to detail and security  
- **Human** – focused on real people, not just technology

## 🧰 Technology

Our stack evolves with every project, but commonly includes:
- **Next.js**, **React**, **TailwindCSS**  
- **Node.js**, **Spring Boot**, **GraphQL**  
- **Docker**, **CI/CD Pipelines**, **Cloud Deployment**

We aim to balance cutting-edge innovation with proven, stable technologies.
